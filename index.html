Why do we need Magnetic Characterization in EV & Battery Manufacturing

Electric vehicles operate with high currents, fast-switching power electronics, and compact battery systems, magnetic behavior directly impacts BMS accuracy, EMI/EMC compliance, safety, and reliability. From battery pack current sensor validation to magnetic material characterization in motors and power electronics, magnetic characterization is essential to ensure accurate sensing, controlled interference, and dependable EV performance. To address these critical requirements, we present below a selection of our standard magnetic measurement solutions for EV applications.
